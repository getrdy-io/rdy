- Checks now reach the machine only through a per-check probe handle. Every file read, directory listing, stat and command is recorded in the report's action log, with secrets in the entries redacted and counted.
- The probe refuses reads of `/etc/shadow`, `/etc/sudoers*`, ssh and kube directories, private key files, `/proc/<pid>/environ` and its kin, and `/dev`. A call made after its check has ended is refused and logged.
