- Redact what a size cap cut. A file read, tail read, stdout or stderr that hit its cap loses the partial line at the cut, and an unfinished private key is removed to the end of the text, so half a secret cannot slip past the rules. Secrets rdy handles (the kubeconfig token, the proxy password, the value of a secret-looking environment variable) are removed wherever they appear, in the report, the action log, logs and errors.
