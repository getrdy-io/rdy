- Redact what a size cap cut. A file read, tail read, stdout or stderr that hit its cap loses the partial line at the cut (or, with no newline, a window up to the nearest separator), and an unfinished private key is removed to the end of the text, so half a secret cannot slip past the rules. Secrets rdy handles (the kubeconfig token and key, the proxy password, the value of a secret-looking environment variable) are removed wherever they appear, also percent-encoded and JSON-escaped, in the report, the action log, logs and errors. A secret the set cannot hold is counted as `known_secret_refused` in the report.
