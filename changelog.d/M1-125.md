- Add `rdy spec keygen` and `rdy spec sign`. `keygen` writes `<name>.key` and `<name>.pub` and never replaces a file. The secret key is private (mode 600) and encrypted with scrypt at the cost stock minisign uses, unless you pass `--unencrypted`. `sign` lints the spec, refuses one with errors or a key that others can read, signs the exact bytes, checks its own signature and writes `<spec>.minisig`. Stock minisign verifies the signature. The password comes from the prompt, `--password-file` (`-` for standard input) or `RDY_KEY_PASSWORD`. A secret key that asks for more than the stock cost is refused. `sign` warns when the key has no password.
