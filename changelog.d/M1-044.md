- A check can reach the machine only through its probe. The build now fails if a check crate opens a file, runs a program, opens a socket, starts a thread, reads the environment or prints to the terminal on its own, and CI proves that every banned item fails the build. A check crate that depends on a crate such as `socket2` or `walkdir` fails the dependency check too. The compiler also refuses any attempt to switch the ban off, even one hidden in a macro.
