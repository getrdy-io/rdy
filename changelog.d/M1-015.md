- `install.sh` now checks the cosign signature of `SHA256SUMS` before it trusts any hash, and stops (exit 1, nothing installed) on a bad signature, a wrong signer or caller, a missing bundle, or no cosign 3.1.3 or newer. `RDY_INSECURE_SKIP_SIGNATURE=1` skips only the signature check, with a warning. The sha256 check always runs.
