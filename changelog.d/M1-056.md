- Redact what rdy prints. Log lines (`RUST_LOG`), panic text and error messages now pass through the redactor before they reach stderr, so a secret in a log field, an environment variable or a kubeconfig is shown as `[REDACTED:...]`. A panic prints two lines (`error:` and `fix:`) instead of the default Rust text, and exits 70. Without `RUST_LOG`, only warnings show and noisy libraries (hyper, h2, rustls, tower, kube) stay at warn.
