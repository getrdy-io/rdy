- The HTML report now carries the full result as a data block (`<script type="application/json" id="rdy-result" data-sha256="...">`). It holds the same bytes as the `.json` file, with `<`, `>` and `&` escaped, and the attribute is their SHA-256. A hostname such as `</script><img onerror=x>` stays text on the page and comes back unchanged from the data.
