- Verify spec signatures in `rdy-spec`. `load()` changed signature: it now takes the signature file, a trust store and a policy, checks the minisign signature on the raw bytes, then the signed product, and only then parses. `SpecError::LoadNotWired` is gone. The new public `rdy_spec::sig` module holds `TrustStore`, `Policy`, `PublicKey` and `SignatureError`, and `LoadedSpec` gains `signature()` and `sha256()`. A tampered, unsigned or wrongly signed spec is refused before any YAML is read.
