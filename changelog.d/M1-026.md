- A spec is refused with exit 64 when it has no checks, more than 256 checks, or names more than 64 distinct hosts across `net.egress`, `net.tls_intercept` and `net.dns`. An unknown key in `metadata` or `bundle` now says which block it is in. A `bundle.collect` item that is not `journal:<duration>`, `k8s:events` or `file:` with an absolute path and no `..` is refused.
- `rdy run` and `rdy verify` refuse a spec whose `metadata.expires` has passed (`spec_expired`, both dates in the message). `rdy-spec` adds `load_at` to pass the clock.
- Kubernetes names and label-key prefixes are checked one dot-separated part at a time, and `net.dns` and `expect_in` have more refuse cases.
