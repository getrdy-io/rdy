- New check `net.proxy`: reads the proxy variables, the systemd drop-ins of Docker, containerd and the kubelet, and the `proxies` of `daemon.json`. It warns when a daemon does not see the proxy and prints the drop-in to create, reports whether `NO_PROXY` covers what the spec requires, warns on a PAC address (never fetched), warns when `HTTPS_PROXY` and `https_proxy` (or the `HTTP` or `NO_PROXY` pair) differ, and blocks when the proxy cannot be reached. A proxy password is never printed.
- `rdy run` now reads `NO_PROXY` (either case). `net.egress` with `via_proxy: auto` connects hosts that `NO_PROXY` lists direct, and says which entry did it. When an `auto` endpoint fails through the proxy and a direct connection works, the result says so.
