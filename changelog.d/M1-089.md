- Every check now carries a title and a remediation in its `CheckInfo` (`meta: CheckMeta`). This is a new field on a public `rdy-engine` type. `cargo xtask checks` lists each registered check with them and fails on an empty one.
- The host stubs now give the same "install a newer rdy" fix as the planner, from the now-public `rdy_engine::plan::NEWER_RDY`.
